## LinkedIn (post with the 4:5 video)

I built a TikTok for brain games. Except it ends, has no ads, and every "reel" is a game you can actually play.

Meet Project Utopia 🪐

You scroll through live, animated game previews. Tap Play and you're in the game in about a second. Tap Exit and you land back on exactly the card you left.

There are 20 games so far, and each one is designed around a different ability:
🌸 Echo Garden: repeat the melody the garden sings (working memory)
🌊 Tidal Beat: keep the rhythm after the music drops (timing)
💡 Lumen: route light through a floating temple (planning)
🐟 Upstream: follow the crowned koi, ignore the rest (attention under conflict)
🪷 Still Water: a breath-paced reset (calm focus)
…and 15 more.

A few things I cared about:
→ It should feel like Monument Valley, not a medical website. Every shape, texture and sound is generated in the browser, with no stock art.
→ Honest science. Each game is inspired by a real research paradigm, and none of them claim to "boost your IQ". Practice isn't treatment, and the About page says so.
→ Respect for attention. The feed has an end. There are no streaks to guilt you and no notifications.
→ Accessible by default: high contrast, reduced motion, relaxed timing, captions and full keyboard play, all applied across every game.

I built it solo (well, with Claude as my pair programmer 😄): React, Three.js / React Three Fiber, PixiJS and the Web Audio API, running as an installable PWA.

The plan is to grow it to 100+ games, and eventually open it up so researchers, designers and developers can contribute their own.

Play it free in your browser: [LIVE LINK]
Code: https://github.com/1sakshm/project-utopia

I'd love to know which game you'd want next, and which one you couldn't put down 👇

## Posting tips
- Upload the video natively (not as a link) on both platforms. Native video gets far more reach.
- LinkedIn: put the link in the post (or the first comment if you find reach drops). Pin a comment asking "Which game should I build next?"
- X: post the thread with the video on tweet 1 and the link on the last tweet.
- Best first frame: the video opens on the planet logo and headline, which works as a thumbnail. On LinkedIn you can also upload `utopia-logo-2160.png` as a custom thumbnail.
